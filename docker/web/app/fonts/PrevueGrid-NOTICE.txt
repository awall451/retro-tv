PrevueGrid.ttf and PrevueGrid-Pixelated.ttf
(c) 2014 Rudy Valencia. A TrueType conversion of the Prevue Channel / TV Guide
Channel listings font (the Amiga "Prevue Grid" software), published by its
author on the Prevue Guide forums (ariweinstein.com, "New Prevue Fonts",
April 2014) without stated licence terms, and redistributed in the MIT-licensed
recreation at https://github.com/rickz0rz/guide, where these files come from.
Used here for the TV Guide Channel page (tvguide.html) of a private project.
The rating badges (NR, G, PG, PG-13, NC-17, R, ADULT, TV-Y … TV-MA) are glyphs
at U+F008 to U+F015, closed captions at U+F01C.
